TUE THANH ASSEMBLY HALL
COFFEE TABLE BOOK

This Book is inspired by the Tue Thanh Assembly Hall—an architectural treasure built by the Chinese community in Cho Lon. Just like the hall itself, the design blends symbolism, history, and artistry into one form. The tabletop draws from the patterns of roof tiles and decorative motifs, reinterpreted into a contemporary surface where people can gather, rest, and connect.

MASS MEDIA CLUB'S
PHOTOSHOOT POST

An appreciation carousel celebrating every member of the RMIT Mass Media Club 2024-2025 after their photoshoot. Each department—Admin, Operation, ER, HR, Creative, Marketing, Media, and Design—gets its own retro desktop-themed slide.

TINH YEU WEBSITE

Inspired by Cá Hồi Hoang’s song of the same name, this project explores the nostalgia of Vietnamese weddings in the early 2000s, when celebrations felt simpler and more intimate. Drawing from the creator’s parents’ wedding, the work captures familiar rituals and close family gatherings. It reflects a sense of love shared not only between two individuals, but also between two families coming together.

BUG'S RECORDS

The work explores how personal taste and memories can be expressed through music and customization. Inspired by early internet aesthetics, collectible CDs, and playful DIY culture, the project creates a dreamy interactive environment where users leave their own visual "trace" through customized CD artworks. Using pastel gradients, illustrated insects, and layered graphic patterns, the experience transforms music sharing into a personal form of self-expression.
